# Cheese Louise: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026. Sycamore Hill Studios ("we") makes Cheese Louise: Roll & Smash ("the game"). This page says what data the game handles and why.

## No accounts or servers of our own
We don't run an account system or a game server, and the game has no sign-up. Your progress is saved on your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes your progress.

## Ads (Google AdMob)
The game only shows an ad when you tap a button that offers a stated reward for watching one, for example doubling the curds from a run or speeding up a cheese in your cellar. There are no banner ads, no ads between runs and no ads that start by themselves. Saying no to an ad never costs you anything.

Ads are served by Google AdMob, which collects and shares data to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud: your device's advertising ID, approximate location (from your IP address), how you interact with ads and the app, and diagnostic data. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy choices, which is also where players in US states with privacy laws can opt out of personalized ads. We ask Google for ads rated suitable for general audiences (G). If you own the Supporter Pass or are a Big Cheese Club member, rewards are given instantly and no ad is shown. AdMob's data use is described in [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and at [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Purchases (Google Play)
Truffles, the Supporter Pass, season passes, cosmetic packs and the Big Cheese Club subscription are sold through Google Play. We receive a record that a purchase was made (the product and a purchase token) so the game can deliver it; we never see your name, email address or payment details. Google's privacy policy applies to the payment. You can manage or cancel a subscription in the Google Play Store app under Payments & subscriptions.

## Notifications
If you allow notifications, the game can tell you when a cheese in your cellar has aged, when your cellar is full, or when a new season starts. You can turn them off at any time in the game's Settings or in Android's settings.

## What we don't do
We don't collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, files or precise location. The game asks for no location permission. We don't use third-party analytics, and we don't sell personal data.

## Children
The game is not directed at children under 13. Ads in the game are limited to content rated for general audiences.

## Deleting your data
Because your progress lives only on your device, deleting the game or clearing its data in Android's settings removes it. Data held by Google for ads or purchases can be managed from your [Google Account](https://myaccount.google.com). If you have a question or request about your data, email us and we'll help.

## Changes
If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
